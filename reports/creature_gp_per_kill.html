Which creatures pay the most GP per kill

Average GP one kill is worth: the gold the creature drops directly, plus the most NPCs would pay for the rest of its loot. Use it to compare hunting targets. It does not say how much gold a world produced, because that also depends on how many players hunt each creature.

Direct GP per kill is the gold the creature drops itself. NPC loot GP per kill is the most NPCs would pay for everything else it drops, so Potential GP per kill is an upper bound that assumes you collect and sell all of it. Values are averages from TibiaWiki loot statistics; a creature with few loot samples is a rougher estimate. Player-market prices are never counted. GP means Tibia gold pieces. TC means Tibia Coins.
